Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

When you use our forms (contact, register, list your business) you may give us your name, phone number, email address and message. We only collect what you choose to type into these forms.

2. How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to connect you with jobs, employees, businesses or services, and to improve the website. We do not sell your personal information.

3. WhatsApp and your browser

Our forms open WhatsApp with your message ready to send. WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy policy. Some details may also be saved in your own browser on your own device.

4. Third parties

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. At the moment we do not run advertising or tracking cookies. If we add tools such as analytics or an account system, we will update this page.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you sent us at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us or write to info@digiserbd.com.
